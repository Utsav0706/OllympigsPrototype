Eight hexes. Do not add any. Image version: `Palette_Reference.png`.

|---|---|---|---|

| Moss Green | `#7C9A6D` | 0.486, 0.604, 0.427 | **Reserved (Silver tier) — do not use for first clear** |
| Ochre | `#D3A95B` | 0.827, 0.663, 0.357 | The first-clear print colour. The stamp |

Rules of thumb:
- Ollypig is in colour from the start; the environment earns its colour.
- Ochre appears only on the stamp — renderers off until `StampOchre()`.
- Vermillion marks what the player should act on (prompt, seal), nothing else.
